Hello ate Pam! Siguro nababasa mo na to during your flight or maybe pag nakaland ka na jan.

I just wanna say that I'm proud of you always and I'm happy for you kasi nakaalis ka na ng bansa.

I'm just sad (and konting tampo siguro konti lang naman) kasi sa birthday ko pa natapat yung flight mo and may pasok din ako so I can't hatid you sa airport, but I know naman na ayaw mo rin kasi baka hilahin kita at sumama ka HAHAHAHA de joke lang.

Pagbalik mo dito, BSIT graduate na ko at pwede na tayong mag-travel!

I remember dati sinasabi mo sakin na everything is temporary even our friendship, but I don't want na maniwala don, kasi it will be one of my biggest heartbreaks.

Sabi mo dapat matutunan kong tanggapin na walang permanente sa mundo and I need to learn how to be independent.

For more than 3 years, nakasama kita, di mo ko pinabayaan, you took care of me, and you were always there. I took that for granted. I thought we still had so much time together, I thought nandito ka throughout my college life.

But I'm not guilt-tripping you, I'm just pouring it all out kasi I'm really sad knowing na you'll leave, and I don't even know kelan ka babalik. Sabi mo 3 years, but I doubt that baka kasi bigla ka nang mag-enjoy jan.

Nung victory ball, I was so happy pa kasi miss na miss kita non, but the moment na sinabi mong it will be your last here kaya mag-e-enjoy ka na, biglang nawala yung smile ko.

Although I'm genuinely happy for you kasi its your dream, there's still a part of me na I don't want you to go. But again, who am I to stop you diba? Well, I'm just here to support you naman ate.

Sadyang mami-miss ko lang talaga yung mga araw na kasama ka pa namin dito, pati yung mga panahong pwede kitang yayain kahit anong oras nang hindi na kailangang dumaan pa sa screen ng cellphone para lang magkausap tayo.

Ang hirap lang isipin na simula ngayon, sa chat at tawag na lang muna tayo magkakasama.

I will never forget our whole day bonding nung Sept 25, nung nag-gala tayo sa MOA.

Sobrang gulat pa nga ako nung bigla mo akong binigyan ng early birthday gift sa Uniqlo na t-shirt, tapos natuwa pa ako lalo nung nag-matching tayong dalawa!

We did so much that day, pinanood natin si Bam, naglakad-lakad tayo nang naglakad hanggang sa mapagod, took so many pictures para may remembrance tayo, tapos nilibre mo pa ako sa Yoshinoya.

Habang kumakain tayo, ang dami mong binigay na advices sakin that felt like a proper pep talk before leaving. Naalala ko pa nung pinanood natin sa phone ko yung Forgotten Island kasi mahal yung ticket kaya di namin binili, tapos hindi na namin natapos kasi kailangan na naming umakyat para manood kay Bam hays.

Sobrang saya ko nung araw na 'yun, kaya nung pauwi na ako, medyo mabigat na agad sa pakiramdam knowing na mamimiss ko yung mga simpleng gala at tawanan nating ganun.

Tapos nung last Monday, yun na pala yung very last time that we'd see each other.

Coincident lang na nagkasabay tayo sa byahe, pero siguro yun na talaga yung sinasabi ni Lord na mag-bond tayo para sa last time.

Nung nakita kitang nag-wave goodbye mula sa jeep, sinubukan ko talagang pigilin yung tears ko para hindi na lumala pa.

Ramdam na ramdam ko na sa katawan ko na yun na talaga yung last time na mahahawakan at makikita kita physically before you fly out.

Doon ko mas na-realize kung gaano kahirap yung adjustment, from being used to dropping by or meeting up whenever I want, ngayon eh magkaiba na nga talaga tayo ng place at masusubukan na yung pagiging malayo sa isa't isa.

I was also thinking about how much I'm going to miss the simple things, like bigla na lang akong pupunta sa inyo, our casual galas, and especially yung pag-simba natin together.

Simple lang naman yung mga yun sa iba, but to me, those simple moments meant everything.

Ewan ko ba, I got so used to having you around all the time, kaya nung narealize ko na malayo ka na, parang naging sobrang tahimik bigla ng paligid ko.

Tapos mapapatingin na lang ako sa mga usual spots natin kasi hahanapin ko talaga yung mga kwento at kakulitan mo.

The hardest part to accept right now is that whenever I'm going through something heavy o kaya kapag gusto lang kita makasama at kwentuhan nang personal, hindi na kita basta-basta mapupuntahan o mahaharap tulad ng dati.

Pero despite all of that, I am still super thankful to God kasi binigay niya sakin yung 3 solid years na nakasama kita nang malapit.

Ang dami kong natutunan sayo about how to be independent, how to handle things on my own, and how to stay grounded, kaya dadalhin ko talaga lahat ng mga bilin at aral mo habang pinapatuloy ko rito ang college life ko.

Even if you always remind me na kailangan ko nang matutong mag-isa, please know na ikaw pa rin yung isa sa mga naging safest space at best friend ko.

That won't change no matter how many miles away you are from me.

Kaya when you get there, please promise me na aalagaan mo rin yung sarili mo ha?

Just enjoy the process, explore new places, make new memories, and please don't hesitate to message me whenever you feel overwhelmed o kaya homesick, dahil kahit anong oras, sasagutin at babasahin ko mga kwento mo.

I'll wrap this up na baka sabihin mo na namang ang drama ko masyado HAHAHA.

Thank you so much for everything, ate Pam. For the memories, the care, and for being such an amazing and solid friend to me throughout the years.

Mag-iingat ka jan palagi ha, huwag kang magpapakapagod nang sobra! Super proud na proud ako sa'yo, always and forever. I love you and I'll miss you so much, ate Pam!


See You Soon my Ate!
